# ANCHOR — Product context

## What it is

ANCHOR is a child care and activity platform for daycare, sports programs, and camps. Parents get a single timeline of their child's day away from home. Staff publish voice-powered daily reports, photos, and incident documentation.

## Audience

- **Primary:** Affluent, time-constrained parents who expect premium communication from programs they trust.
- **Secondary:** Program owners and coaches who need fast daily reporting without admin overhead.

## Positioning

Calm luxury healthcare-adjacent trust — not playful ed-tech, not corporate SaaS. Works alongside Brightwheel and TeamSnap; ANCHOR owns daily story, safety, care, and enrollment narrative.

## Marketing surfaces (Persuade mode)

Homepage, header, and footer must earn trust quickly and route visitors to **For programs** (sign-up) or **Parent sign in**.

## Non-negotiables

- Child safety and parent visibility in every message.
- Accessible contrast, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support.
- Real product truth only — no fabricated metrics or partner logos.
